Papers and preprints

E3Sense: Head-Confined Multimodal Sensing of Learner Engagement

Co-first author · 2026
Submitted to ACM CHI · Preprint available

A head-mounted platform integrating EEG, eye tracking, and forehead EDA. A 30-participant study collected 450 engagement ratings; evaluation on 15 held-out forehead-sensing participants achieved 75.0% balanced within-one-level prediction versus a 63.0% majority-rating baseline.

arXiv preprint

Multimodal Engagement Decoding During Live Classroom Lectures

Co-first author · 2026
Submitted to IEEE ICASSP · Poster presented at CNS 2026

A 12-participant live-lecture study synchronizing scalp EEG, wearable EEG, eye tracking, and instructor speech. A multimodal CRNN using headband EEG, gaze, and the speech envelope achieved MAE 0.628 and 91.2% balanced within-one accuracy on held-out participants.

EMG-FM-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Foundation Model Transfer and Adaptation on Electromyography

Co-author · 2026
Under review at ICLR 2027 · Preprint available

A unified benchmark spanning 20 public datasets, more than one million EMG segments, and nine pretrained foundation models. My contributions include standardized preprocessing and evaluation across transfer, cross-user generalization, few-shot adaptation, and EMG-to-text decoding.
